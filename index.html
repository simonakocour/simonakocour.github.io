Simona Kocour

I am a PhD student at the Czech Institute of Informatics, Robotics and Cybernetics (CIIRC) at the Czech Technical University (CTU). My PhD is part of the ELLIS program under the supervision of Dr. Torsten Sattler and I am co-supervised by Prof. Dr. Siyu Tang.

My research topic is 3D scene understanding and representation. I am part of the Spatial Intelligence Group in Prague.

I received my Bachelors and Masters degree in Mathematics from Masaryk University. After the Masters, I worked as a Data Scientist, and then as a Machine Learning Researcher for 3 years.
